About Me

I believe that everyone who runs is a runner and everyone who wants a coach deserves one. I send training on a weekly basis to make sure that it adapts to meet your needs in real time and I am available daily for as needed check ins. I offer a sliding scale of $50-$70 per month with full scholarships always available.
